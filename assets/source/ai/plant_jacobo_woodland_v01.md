## Exact prompt

Create a production game sprite atlas, transparent background, 2 by 2 equally sized cells with generous empty margins and no text. Four distinct natural roadside plants for a classic 16-bit pixel RPG, front / slightly elevated orthographic view. Top left: broad deciduous tree with visible crooked brown trunk and layered rounded dark green leafy crown. Top right: slender young deciduous tree with an airy irregular crown and visible branches. Bottom left: large wild fern clump with clearly separated fronds. Bottom right: irregular bramble shrub with small pale flowers and tall grass around its base. True chunky low resolution pixel art, crisp large pixel clusters, limited palette dark navy purple shadows, deep teal green, muted olive and a few warm gold highlights; natural vegetation, not bright topiary. Match reference mood but MUCH coarser, classic readable sprite shapes equivalent to 48x56px each. No ground plate, no pots, no rectangular hedge, no cast shadows outside each sprite. All 4 complete silhouettes isolated in cells, with transparent alpha between leaves and fronds, zero background. Assets are intended for Godot roadside foliage and will be normalized offline.
