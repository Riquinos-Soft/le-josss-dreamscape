# Bar Scandale source notes

Five developer-supplied interior photographs, 2026-10-15. Local attachment names
start `orca-paste-1792088066619`, `1792088071429`, `1792088076036`,
`1792088080542`, `1792088085456`. These contain HEIF data despite PNG extensions.
Inspection conversions remain in ignored `build/verification/scandale/references`;
photos and depicted people's images are not shipped as runtime textures.
No scan, survey or measured dimensions was supplied. All dimensions below are
photo-derived gameplay estimates, not surveyed reconstruction.

Defining landmarks: long wooden counter with return; suspended grey metal rack
with green bottles and inverted glasses; cream walls covered in photo/poster
clusters; terracotta floor; pink mirror ball; small blue TV over the rear passage.
A front-door view appears in photo 5; photos 1/3 face toward the rear passage.
Photo 2 shows the counter return, cooler and fan. The blond/blue-shirt bartender
is the requested visual reference; the fictional NPC has no real name or biography.

The authored room is approximately 6 × 10 metres. Width is expanded for the existing
player capsule and a clear customer aisle. The camera-facing walls/ceiling are cut
away; hidden circulation and the rear door's destination are not invented as rooms.
The rear door remains decorative. The visible front threshold returns to the map.
Original abstract pixel poster panels stand in for the collages and branded posters;
no real photographs, commercial labels, poster lettering or likenesses are copied.
See `game/world/scandale.gd` for editable geometry and material parameters.
