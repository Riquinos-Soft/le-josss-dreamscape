## Exact prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a spectacular widescreen background illustration for the areas outside a floating island game map. Reference image is the game's title screen, STYLE REFERENCE ONLY. Match its richly detailed painterly pixel-art fantasy, midnight indigo and violet clouds, golden lit windows, monumental stone castles on floating craggy islands, impossibly long arched bridges, sparkling nebulae and luminous moon. Compose a breathtaking panoramic dream realm with several large readable castle islands distributed across BOTH SIDES and LOWER HALF, distant smaller islands fading into atmospheric clouds. Center can be calmer because gameplay covers it. Full bleed landscape 16:9, no title, no text, no logo, no foreground people, no village foreground or doors, no simple polygonal 3D shapes. This is a finished illustrated background, deep layered atmospheric perspective, intricate silhouette and warm/cool lighting like the reference.
